# discolean

`discolean` is the Lake package of DisCoPy's Lean, built on
[Mathlib](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4): its library
`DisCoLean` holds the categories of [`discopy.abc`](../discopy/abc.py)
compiled to Lean, the proof that diagrams are the arrows of the free category
with each structure, one module of DisCoPy at a time, and the models of the
algorithms of [`discopy/egraph.py`](../discopy/egraph.py), linked to the Python
by a differential test, and the terms of `cat` and `monoidal`.

## The categories of `discopy.abc`

[`DisCoLean/ABC.lean`](DisCoLean/ABC.lean) is generated by
`python -m discopy.lean`, see [`discopy/lean.py`](../discopy/lean.py): each
class is a structure of the operations its rules state, with their typing
laws, and of the laws its axioms state, read off the declarations by
evaluating them on symbols. Morphisms are untyped, as in the Python. The
classes it does not compile yet are listed at its top, each with the reason.
Regenerate it after a change to `discopy.abc`: `test/lean.py` fails until it
is.

The theory of a module is the laws of an abstract class its diagrams do not
declare broken, `DisCoLean.ABC.monoidal` for `monoidal.Diagram`, and its
coherence theorem says the diagrams are the arrows of the free model of it.
Each compiled class also has a `Functor` structure, the maps that preserve its
operations. For `monoidal`, [`DisCoLean/Coherence.lean`](DisCoLean/Coherence.lean)
proves the whole theorem: the models of `DisCoLean.ABC.monoidal` are the
strict premonoidal categories (`toABC` and `ofABC`), and `Diagram.free` says a
compiled `MonoidalCategory.Functor` from Diagram(S) to any model is given by
where it sends the generating objects and the boxes, and by nothing else.

## The model of `discopy.egraph`

A pure model of `partition`, the pure `EGraph` and the `Builder`, with
theorems proved on the model. Nothing here is a proof about the Python:
the two are linked by a differential test, the model run as an oracle against
the Python on generated cases.

The model mirrors `discopy/egraph.py` as of `45f9200`. A change to the
algorithms there is followed here, or the differential test says so.

What is modelled, and what is not:

- `partition n pairs` is modelled by its result, the least spider related to
  each spider, not by its union-find: the model rewrites the whole list of
  classes for each pair.
- An e-graph is a list of nodes and the class of each spider: a node is a
  box, given by a number, whether it is deterministic, and its input and
  output spiders. There is no `Hypergraph`: no domain or codomain, and
  left-monogamy and acyclicity are neither modelled nor needed.
- `rebuild` runs on fuel, the sum of the classes, where the Python loops
  until nothing changes: `rebuild_congruent` shows the fuel is enough.
- `add` leaves out the `partition` of `EGraph.__init__`, which changes nothing
  on normal classes.
- The `Builder` keeps `least` and `parents` in arrays where the Python has
  dictionaries, has no `heads` or `known`, since a node carries the number of
  its box, and cuts `union` in three methods, `union`, `join` and `link`. Its
  loops, in `root` and `rebuild`, run on fuel, which `building` shows is
  enough. That law, a builder freezes into what its operations give in turn,
  is proved for a valid e-graph whose nodes read spiders that are there, and
  for the histories the Python accepts: each box added reads spiders that are
  there and has the number of outputs of its head, each merge relates spiders
  that are there.
- Spiders are natural numbers and have no type: `partition` takes any pairs,
  in the Python as in the model, and it is `EGraph.__init__` that refuses
  classes relating spiders of two types. A valid e-graph of the model has
  one number of outputs for each head, which the Python reads off the type.

## Diagrams are the morphisms of a premonoidal category

[`DisCoLean/Statements/Premonoidal.lean`](DisCoLean/Statements/Premonoidal.lean) models
[`monoidal.Diagram`](../discopy/monoidal.py) as `Diagram.encode` writes it, a
domain and a list of boxes with offsets, and proves that these diagrams are a
strict premonoidal category in which interchange holds for two boxes only
when they are one and the same scalar: with any other box, Diagram(S) is not
monoidal. It is the free one: generating objects and boxes sent into a strict
premonoidal category extend to exactly one strict premonoidal functor. The
model mirrors
`discopy/monoidal.py` as of `c842a24`.

- Morphisms are untyped, with `dom` and `cod`, as in the Python, so that
  `(X ⊗ Y) ⋊ f = X ⋊ (Y ⋊ f)` is an equality of morphisms with no transport
  along `(X ++ Y) ++ Z = X ++ (Y ++ Z)`. Composition is total and every law
  about it assumes its arguments composable: where the Python raises, the
  model returns its first argument and nothing is said of it.
- A functor is tested against `monoidal.Functor` into diagrams only, where
  it sends each box to a diagram; the theorem is about any codomain.
- One box per layer: a `Layer` with several boxes, which `foliation` makes
  and `encode` flattens, is not modelled.
- No colours: types are lists of wires, the free monoid, where a coloured
  `Ty` is a path and its tensor is partial.
- `Sum`, `Bubble` and the subclasses of `Diagram` are not modelled.

## Terms are diagrams

[`DisCoLean/Statements/Terms.lean`](DisCoLean/Statements/Terms.lean) models the
kappa calculus of [`cat.TermBase`](../discopy/cat.py) and the planar linear one
of [`monoidal.TermBase`](../discopy/monoidal.py), as of `b66485d`. Nothing links
the model to the Python but its definitions: there is no differential test.

- Terms are nameless and indexed by their boundary: in `cat` a context has
  one variable, so that `Open S a b` is `x : a ⊢ e : b`; in `monoidal` a
  variable is where it stands in the context, `Open S Γ b` is `Γ ⊢ e : b`.
  Alpha-equivalent terms are equal and substitution captures nothing, where
  the Python names its variables (which is how `rel-int/discopy-next#704`
  showed up).
- In `cat`, the five laws of `cat.TermBase` are proved: `eta`,
  `normal_form_soundness`, `normal_form_idempotence`,
  `normal_form_completeness` and `reading`. Evaluation into any category
  factors through the free one, so beta reduction is sound under every
  functor.
- In `monoidal`, only terms with no stack and boxes with one output: a term
  evaluates into Diagram(S) of the premonoidal model, its arguments the last
  one first as the Python does, from its context to its output, eta holds for
  a box, and a diagram with one output reads as a term that evaluates back
  to it, box for box. The reading gives a `let` to every box, the last one
  included, where the Python gives none to the last. No normal form, no
  kappa with a stack or offset, no boxes of several outputs, and none of
  `symmetric` or `markov`.
