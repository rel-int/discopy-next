# 9. A bang at every level, as feedback is

## Context

[#686](https://github.com/rel-int/discopy-next/pull/686) moved the free linear category to
`discopy.bang`, a side branch above `monoidal`: only a plain monoidal diagram could be banged, the
exchange of a banged wire was never the swap, and the terms of `bang` did not mix with those of
other levels. toumix, asked where the bang fits in the hierarchy, ruled on 2026-11-21: "I think
bang should go everywhere the same way feedback goes everywhere, the user can always decide not
to use it". He approved the design on #686 the same day.

## Decision

`bang.Diagram` is a base of the four diagrams that subclass `monoidal.Diagram` directly,
`spacial`, `braided`, `feedback` and `biclosed`, and `bang.Ty` of their types, so that every level
above is a linear category. `monoidal` has no bang, as it has no feedback. Each level has a
`BangOb`, a `Bang` and the structural boxes of its own, generated by `bang.structural_wires` and
`bang.structural_boxes`. From `symmetric` up the exchange is the swap and no `Exchange` box is
generated; `braided` keeps its own, which is not a braid. From `markov` up the bang is still a free
comonad, not the identity: its contraction and weakening are the copy and the discard of a banged
wire, and a banged box is deterministic, so that copying is natural on it and not on a box that
samples. `abc.LinearCategory` does not change and no other `abc` class inherits it.

## Consequences

- A concrete category made of free diagrams, e.g. `tensor.Diagram`, has the free `.bang()`: a
  functor into it sends a bang through its `ob_map` and `ar_map`, since its bang does not build
  its own arrows (`bang.Functor.has_bang`).
- A box of any level from `spacial` up called on terms is its constant applied to them.
- A banged wire has no adjoint: from `rigid` up `x.bang().l` raises, and so do the rotations of the
  structural boxes.
- The laws of `abc.LinearCategory` are declared failing on every free category, including those
  where the exchange laws are instances of the symmetry.
