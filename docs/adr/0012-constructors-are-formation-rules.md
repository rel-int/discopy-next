# 12. Constructors are formation rules of the objects

## Context

A category's morphisms are derived from its rules, e.g. `cut` and `mix`, which is how
`cat.Arrow.search` and the rule matrix generate them. Its objects were not: `Tensor`, `L`, `R`,
`Delay`, `Over`, `Under`, `Bang`, `Unit` and `Repeat` were expressions in the signatures of rules
and axioms only, built by their `form`. A sampler that derives both, e.g. a Boltzmann sampler on
the sequent calculus of a category, had no declaration of the objects to read. A first cut
declared the formers on the categories, concluding objects; joseph ruled that they belong on
the types instead.

## Decision

The objects of a category are the morphisms of a category of their own, a `ColouredMonoid`
between colours, and a constructor is a rule of it. `Constructor` subclasses `Rule`, and
the constructor `K` decorates its rule, `@K`, on the class of objects whose structure it is: `cut` of a
coloured monoid is the `Tensor` and `ax` the `Unit`, with `form_repeat`; a `ResiduatedMonoid` has
`form_over` and `form_under`, a `Pregroup` `form_l` and `form_r`, a `DelayMonoid` `form_delay`
and a `BangMonoid` `form_bang`. `Testable.constructors` lists the rules of a class that are
constructors.

## Consequences

- Objects are generated by the same machinery as morphisms, rules with `Hom` premises and
  conclusions, and the rule matrix checks every former of every enrolled class of objects.
- A class whose objects lack a former declares it inapplicable, as `interaction.Ty` does for pair
  types.
- `abc.DelayMonoid` and `abc.BangMonoid` are new: `feedback.Ty` and `bang.Ty` take them, and
  `biclosed.Ty` takes `ResiduatedMonoid`, whose methods it already had.
- `discopy.lean` leaves formation rules out: each is an operation its structures already have.
- The equations between objects, e.g. `x.r.l == x`, stay axioms of their own.
