# DisCoPy Entity Contributor License Agreement

This agreement is between an organisation, such as an employer, a university or a funder, called
"the Entity" below, and DisCoPy, a nonprofit association under the French law of 1 July 1901,
[legal name, RNA number and registered address], called "the Association". It is for an Entity
that holds rights in what some people create, so that those people can contribute to DisCoPy.

## 1. Who it covers

The Entity lists, in a schedule to this agreement that it may update in writing, the people
("Designated Contributors") whose contributions it covers. Each of them also accepts the
individual [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md).

## 2. Terms

For every contribution a Designated Contributor submits while listed, the Entity grants the
Association exactly what a contributor grants under sections 2 to 5 of the individual agreement,
as if the Entity had written that contribution, and receives the licence back of its section 3.
Section 6, the Association's commitment to an open-source licence, binds the Association towards
the Entity too.

## 3. What the Entity confirms

The Entity has the right to grant these rights, and the person who signs for it may bind it.

## 4. Law

This agreement is governed by French law.
